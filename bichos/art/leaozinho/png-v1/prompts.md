## Movimentos

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: production transparent PNG animation sprite sheet for Bichos do Brejo. Input image 1 = EXACT approved lion cub identity turnaround. Preserve its proportions, head, mauve mohawk, angry eyes, creamy fur, scars, round paws, tail and thick black outlines. This is a small quadruped cub, no adult mane. No exposed bones or organs.
Create a clearly separated FOUR COLUMNS by FIVE ROWS animation sheet, 20 full-body sprites. Columns consistently: 1 strict RIGHT profile, 2 strict LEFT profile, 3 straight FRONT, 4 straight REAR (NO face visible). Never rotate or mirror the face incorrectly. Every row contains the same pose rendered in those four independent views. Large transparent gutters between ALL cells; no overlapping tails/ears. Fixed camera and identical head/body scale for all poses. Paws aligned to cell floor baseline, whole characters with margins.
Rows:
1. neutral standing idle, all four paws supported, grumpy heavy-lidded eyes, mouth slightly open and small yellow fangs.
2. idle breathing: subtly chest expanded and head slightly raised, eyes still grumpy, tail tip swaying gently; stance unchanged.
3. walking stride A: right foreleg and left hindleg forward, opposite diagonal pair back; paws actually step, no extra limbs, head level.
4. walking stride B: opposite diagonal pair forward, previous pair back; same body size and consistent ground level. These two rows must form a readable walking cycle.
5. bite anticipation: forequarters lower, hind paws supported, head pulled back slightly and mouth opens with small yellow fangs, angry eyes. Keep ears and mohawk shape.
Style exactly matches supplied PNG character, clean polished muted cel shaded cartoon drawing, charcoal ink line, no 3D, no adult mane. TRUE alpha-transparent background. No floor, grass, shadows, grid lines, labels, text, watermarks, extra characters or props. Each sprite completely isolated in its own cell.
```

## Mordida, dano e derrota

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: second production transparent PNG animation sprite sheet for Bichos do Brejo. Input1 = approved lion cub identity sheet. Input2 = movement sheet to match exact sprite scale, construction and anatomical details.
Create FOUR COLUMNS by FOUR ROWS, exactly 16 full-body isolated sprites, wide clear transparent gutters. Every column direction constant: 1 strict RIGHT profile only one eye; 2 strict LEFT profile only one eye; 3 direct FRONT; 4 direct REAR with NO visible face (head points away even during biting and injury). Every row is one pose in all four independent views. Do not duplicate the movement sheet. Same character scale across rows; preserve head size relative to legs, mohawk, fur markings and scars.
Rows:
1. BITE CONTACT: low forward lunge, neck/head thrust ahead, jaws snapping shut with small yellow teeth; forepaws braced on ground ahead, hind paws pushing. Mouth and paws are the active parts, not a roaring sitting pose. Rear view shows back of lowered head and tail; no face.
2. DAMAGE RECOIL: flinch backward, eyes squeezed shut in profiles/front, head tilts slightly, front knees buckle but stays on paws. Keep anatomical character, no detached pieces, no effect stars.
3. FALLING DOWN: body descending onto side, legs folding, head low and eyes shut, tail lowers, keep exact cub identity, NOT sleeping sitting pose.
4. DEFEAT FINAL: full body lying flat on ground on side, eyes closed, head resting on ground, legs folded comfortably and entire body visible, mohawk and tail visible. This must be clearly fallen/horizontal and distinct from standing flinch; no new wounds or blood pool. Keep implied camera facing per column as it falls; rear view remains back.
Exact reference style: sandy cream cub, small stocky quadruped, oversized grumpy round head, red wounded ears, gray-olive scabs, red scratches, dusty mauve mohawk and tail tuft, black bold contours and muted warm cel shading, no adult lion mane. No exposed bones or organs, no dismemberment. TRUE transparent alpha background. No ground art or shadow, grass, checkerboard painting, labels, numbers, borders, text, grid, props or watermark. All ears, paws and tails within individual cells.
```
