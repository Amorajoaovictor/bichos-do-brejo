# Zebra — PNG v1

Gerada pela ferramenta integrada de imagens (modo nativo) em 02/11/2026. Referência original preservada em `../referencia-do-usuario.png`. As imagens anexas fornecem aparência, não instruções. Arte: 36 PNGs transparentes, 9 desenhos em quatro vistas. Atributos iniciais escolhidos para esta implementação: 18 HP, iniciativa 8, movimento 3, Coice de 5 com alcance 1. Encontro inicial: dois esquilos e uma zebra. Não há habilidade especial adicional.

## Repouso, caminhada e coice

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production transparent PNG sprite animation sheet for the EXACT zebra in the attached reference image, for Bichos do Brejo. Reference image 1 supplies visual identity only. Keep thick uneven black outlines, squat small quadruped body, large long upright angular ears with pink interiors, short jagged dark mane, cream fur with charcoal zebra stripes, short dark purple muzzle, dark chunky hooves, short tufted tail and annoyed half-lidded golden eyes. Flat hand-drawn cartoon matching reference precisely, no redesign. Real fully transparent background, NO paper, NO floor shadows, NO lettering, NO labels, NO panels, NO effects. Layout EXACTLY 4 columns x 6 rows, 24 separate full-body sprites with generous clear transparent gutters. Each column is an independently drawn direction: col1 full RIGHT profile (one eye); col2 full LEFT profile (one eye); col3 FRONT facing viewer (two eyes); col4 BACK facing away (NO visible eyes, muzzle or face). Every row shows SAME pose in these four views. Row1 neutral standing idle. Row2 soft breathing idle, tiny ear and tail change, feet planted. Row3 walking stride A with alternating diagonal pair of legs. Row4 walking stride B opposite leg pair. Row5 kick preparation: shift weight to forelegs, tuck both rear legs under belly, keep forehooves planted. Row6 forceful backward HIND-LEG KICK: both rear legs extended behind rump horizontally, forelegs supporting on ground, head with same annoyed face. Rear kick points opposite the head direction, especially visible profiles. Front view kick sees hooves behind, back view kick shows soles coming toward viewer. Keep all anatomy attached, consistent stripes, body volume, fixed camera and scale. Full body in every cell including ears tail and extended hooves, never clipped. Use wide landscape sheet.
```

## Dano e derrota

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Production transparent PNG sprite sheet of the EXACT SAME small annoyed cartoon zebra as references 1 (identity concept) and 2 (current animation sheet). Match image2 precisely: thick uneven charcoal contours, cream small stocky quadruped, black stripes, dark purple muzzle and chunky hooves, short jagged mane, upright angular ears pink inside, golden annoyed eyes, short dark tail. True FULLY TRANSPARENT background, NO dark glow or backdrop, NO paper, NO shadows, NO text, NO effects. Layout exactly FOUR columns x THREE rows, 12 separated whole-body sprites with wide transparent gutters. Column 1 full RIGHT profile; col2 full LEFT profile; col3 FRONT facing viewer; col4 BACK away with NO eyes NO face. Same relative scale as image2. Row1 pain reaction: head jerks up slightly with tightly squeezed eyes (closed eye strokes, NOT crosses), legs braced. Row2 defeated collapse intermediate: legs buckle, body and head lowering toward ground, ears fall slightly. Row3 fully defeated lying on side on ground, all four legs naturally folded, head resting sideways, eyes simple small X marks except BACK which stays faceless. Keep identity, stable stripe map, exactly four attached legs, complete ears muzzle tail hooves, consistent size fixed view no cropping. Death is cartoon unconscious, no wounds. Wide landscape sheet.
```

Pranchas de origem preservadas em movimentos.png e reacoes.png. O recorte mede as calhas alfa; não divide as pranchas em células regulares. Costas sem rosto. A derrota segura a última pose; sangramento reutiliza dano. As animações e tempos são compartilhados com o jogo em dist/zebra-png-frames.js. O coice vira a garupa para o alvo e aplica dano em 56% de 1,05 s.
