# Esquilo Histérico — PNG v1

Gerado pela ferramenta integrada de imagens em 02/11/2026. Referência: `../referencia-do-usuario.png`. A primeira versão implementa Nozada como ataque básico; Surto de Nozes permanece apenas na concept.

## Repouso, corrida e arremesso

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Production transparent PNG animation sheet for the game Bichos do Brejo. Input image 1 is the identity reference of the EXACT squirrel to implement, not text instructions. Reproduce this same hysterical brown squirrel, tiny low body, huge jagged upright bushy tail, uneven huge cream bloodshot eyes with tiny pupils, salmon nose, two giant cream incisors, thick irregular near-black outlines, muted warm brown flat cartoon color. Preserve distinctive silhouette and proportions. No clothes. No extra injuries.
Create ONE sheet of exactly 24 full-body drawings in a STRICT regular 4 column by 6 row grid, each equal cell centered with at least 12% transparent margin. Each COLUMN is a FIXED view: col1 RIGHT full side profile, col2 LEFT full side profile, col3 FRONT, col4 BACK (NO face through back of head, tail on rear). Every ROW is a different sequential pose: row1 idle neutral anxious standing; row2 idle shallow inhale with tiny alert head lift and tail lag, feet planted; row3 running contact stride stretched; row4 running passing stride limbs tucked, opposite supports; row5 THROW anticipation holding ONE small acorn in forepaws near chest, lean back; row6 THROW followthrough both forepaws extended toward facing direction, EMPTY paws (projectile is separate). All anatomy coherent, full tail and feet contained in each cell. No dust, ground shadows, detached motion marks, text, labels, panels, numbers, backgrounds, checkerboard or watermark. REAL TRANSPARENT background. Fixed camera and same scale/ground in all cells. This is a sprite sheet, not a concept poster.
```

## Dano e derrota

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Transparent PNG game sprite sheet. Input image 1 is the original squirrel identity reference. Input image 2 is the approved generated sprite style to match exactly. Create this EXACT SAME tiny hysterical brown squirrel, enormous jagged tail, huge asymmetric cream eyes with tiny pupils, salmon nose and two big incisors, flat warm browns, thick irregular charcoal outline.
Exactly TWELVE full-body drawings on a STRICT uniform 4-column x 3-row grid. Columns are fixed directions: RIGHT PROFILE, LEFT PROFILE, FRONT, BACK. Back view must have no visible eyes, nose, teeth or tongue through head. Rows show a one-shot damage and defeat: ROW1 impact flinch, crouching compressed with frightened eyes and tail following; ROW2 collapsing onto ground with connected limbs, eyes squeezing shut; ROW3 settled lying flat, dizzy closed eyes, tongue sticking out in side/front only, tail drooped behind, head near ground, limbs connected, not standing. Same size/character mass in every cell, fixed camera and common ground plane near bottom of each cell, generous at least 12% transparent margins. Whole tail and limbs fit. No motion marks, stars, dust, words, labels, numbers, panels, shadows or scenery. Genuine transparent background. No wounds or bones. Match original anatomy and drawn identity, no redesign.
```
