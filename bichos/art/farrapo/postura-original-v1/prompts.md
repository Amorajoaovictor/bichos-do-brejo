# Farrapo — estudos aprovados com a postura original

O usuário aprovou estes dois desenhos e reafirmou que o resultado do jogo deve ser vector art. Os PNGs fornecem referência visual. Corpo e apoio seguem o desenho frontal original do jogo; aparência e acabamento seguem a concept do usuário. Gerados com a ferramenta integrada de imagens.

## Três quartos

```text
Use case: identity-preserve. Make ONE static transparent full-body drawing of Farrapo in a RIGHT-FACING FRONT THREE-QUARTER view, with both eyes visible.
INPUT ROLES AND PRIORITY: Image 1 is the actual current game's original frontal Farrapo and is the EXACT BODY POSTURE, PROPORTIONS AND STANCE reference. Image 2 is the user's concept and supplies facial personality and the hand-drawn finish. Follow image 1 for the body, and the MIDDLE character of image 2 for the small viewing-angle change and face. The user explicitly asked to keep EXACTLY the current game character's posture. Do not turn this into the lower horizontally-bodied normal rabbit from the concept.
Preserve the original game's unusually broad oversized round head directly over its compact UPRIGHT torso, chest and pale belly patch facing diagonally forward. The torso is directly underneath the head rather than extending horizontally behind it. Reproduce the SAME shoulder height, hanging almost-vertical forelimbs on the sides of the torso, their little paws lowered near the base, and SAME short rear legs, foot support and height. Turn this existing connected standing pose into three quarters; do not invent a human biped with long legs or a seated rabbit with a prominent haunch. The far arm/foot is slightly overlapped by the torso; the near forelimb remains clearly visible descending beside the belly as in image 1. Preserve the body's original head-to-torso-to-limb ratios. The silhouette should read like the original game sprite subtly turned, with its taller chest, rather than a rabbit walking on all fours. Do not relocate the support paws or raise the hands into a new pose.
Keep the unmistakable Farrapo face from the concept: enormous unequal startled eyes with tiny black pupils and tired lower eyelid, small pink triangular nose, very short muzzle, connected uneven paired cream incisors, little muted pink cheek scratch, worn cream fur with sparse irregular gray-brown patches. One tall notched ear and one torn folded dusty-rose ear, same anatomical sides. Thick slightly irregular charcoal outlines and simple muted flat cartoon colors like image 2. Small fluffy tail at the rear. Whole character standing neutrally, one character only, fixed camera like the inputs, full ears and feet inside generous margins.
Actual transparent background with alpha. No ground, backdrop, checkerboard, shadows, text, labels, extra views, grid, watermark, animation, mutations, added wounds, blood, organs or exposed bones. This is a posture-preserving turn of the actual game sprite, not a redesign of its body.
```
