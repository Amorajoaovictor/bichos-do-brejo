Maintain the same anatomical asymmetry across all four views. Follow the actual supplied frontal reference exactly: upright notched ear, larger eye and muted pink crossed cheek scar on screen LEFT; folded torn ear and smaller eye on screen RIGHT. The rear view reverses those screen positions: upright ear on screen RIGHT and folded ear on screen LEFT. The right-facing profile matches reference 1; the opposite profile shows the other side, with the smaller eye and no cheek scar. Preserve the recognizable ear shapes and dusty pink interiors. Preserve the approved drawing's expression and personality.
