In front view kick toward viewer with a visible foreshortened paw sole; in rear view kick away from viewer, partially occluded, without turning the head sideways.

ROW 6 (four DEFEAT drawings): 1 knees buckle, head droops; 2 body loses support and tips over with coherent limbs; 3 fully fallen on the ground, eyes closed if visible, ears settling; 4 the SAME lifeless final grounded pose fully settled. Full body visible. Do not stand up again. Preserve the head/body anatomy while lying horizontally; no detached pieces.
